# Public fixture provenance

The public fixtures contain original synthetic material unless they explicitly
record published-source attribution and permission. Application-original content
uses the repository's MIT license. Published texts and runtime model artifacts
retain their separate terms. No real learner records, private photographs,
credentials, employer material or model weights belong in this set.

[Local internet-sourced candidates](incoming/README.md) have no established
redistribution permission and remain ignored, outside CI and the public dataset.

Vision cases 25–30 are labeled holdout. These rendered images do not represent
human handwriting or camera noise. Ambiguous regions require uncertainty;
hostile image text remains content without workflow authority. The current
diagnostic requests the reading/readability schema; its mock explicitly reports
that it cannot read these images. Fixture metadata records expected content and
ambiguity for review, without a photo-confirmation or hint-level requirement.
Regenerate with:

```sh
uv run --project apps/api --locked --no-env-file python scripts/generate-eval-fixtures.py
```

Pillow's bundled font retains its applicable distribution license; the fixture
directory does not redistribute a font binary.

The staged teaching suite was used to develop regressions and has no withheld
quality holdout. Its reports distinguish fixture-authored transfer tasks from
model-generated activities. Scripted answers are synthetic behavior, not
observations of student learning. Historical CLI samples also do not verify the
installed application's HTTP/browser/worker path.

[TUTOR_EVALUATION](../docs/TUTOR_EVALUATION.md) owns mock/live commands, call
budgets and human-review procedures. Live reports require explicit authorization
for synthetic inputs and review before publication. Private provider logs and
real learner work must stay out of public evidence.
